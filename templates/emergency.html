Rapid Emergency Checklist

1. Financial / Banking Fraud

  1. Call your bank's fraud division immediately to freeze debit/credit cards and online banking.
  2. Request an immediate ACH / SWIFT Wire Recall Notice for any transfers made in the last 72 hours.
  3. Notify your national financial intelligence unit.

2. SIM Swap or Account Takeover

  1. If your mobile phone loses service abruptly, call your telecom provider to lock your SIM card.
  2. Revoke active sessions on primary email accounts and update passwords using a separate secure device.
  3. Enable Hardware 2FA (Authenticator App / Security Key) instead of SMS 2FA.

3. Ransomware / Malware Attack

  1. Immediately disconnect the infected device from Wi-Fi, Ethernet networks, and Bluetooth.
  2. Do NOT pay the ransom—payment does not guarantee file decryption and funds criminal organizations.
  3. Preserve a full disk image snapshot for forensic law enforcement inspection.
