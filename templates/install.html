Android – Add to Home Screen

1
Scan the QR code or open the app link in Chrome browser
2
Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) at the top right of Chrome
3
Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app"
4
Tap "Add" to confirm — the app icon will appear on your home screen!

iPhone / iPad – Add to Home Screen

1
Scan the QR code or open the app link in Safari browser (not Chrome)
2
Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4
Tap "Add" — the CyberReport icon will appear on your home screen!
